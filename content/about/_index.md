My name is Tobi, I'm in my 30s and live in mid-west germany, where I was also born. "Ei Gude, wie?" or "Gude" is a universal greeting in the area where I'm living. You usually use this with people you know well and you like, for saying "hello" and "how are you". So it might be translated to something like "Hey buddy, how's it going?". So, buddy, I hope you enjoy your time here.

By profession I am a software engineer focusing on systemsoftware/backend technologies, especially C++, Rust and Python in lowlevel environments. Besides that, automation is one of my focus topics. That's the reason why I'm also pretty good with DevOps topics.

In my free time I like to go cycling (especially Mountain- and Gravelbikes), row regularly on my Waterrower and sometimes play videogames. Besides that of course, I read a lot of fiction, nonfiction and blogs.

## Motivation

I think a lot about different topics related to tech, politics, philisophy and psychology.

Those are also the topics I want to handle. My motivation is basically the same as for every blogger: having a knowledge database for technical stuff, and **getting stuff out of my head**.

**Rumination** is one of my bigger problems - especially ruminating about topics but not being able to turn it into productive substance. I write this blog, because I want to tackle this in a productive way. So my goal is to have a habit to write down some pages about my thoughts if I'm hanging somewhere or not. They might be published or not.
